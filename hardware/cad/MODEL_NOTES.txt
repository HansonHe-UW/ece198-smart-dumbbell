CONCEPT DIMENSIONS
Shell envelope: 280 length x 100 width x 86.603 height.
Buttons and sensor protrusions are outside the shell-envelope dimensions.
Hex head circumradius: 50; across flats: 86.603.
Head depth including cap: 70 each.
Head wall and end cap thickness: 3.
Grip: 140 exposed length, 146 full length, outer diameter 34, inner diameter 24.
Grip insertion opening: diameter 34.4, provisional clearance only.
End caps: four diameter-3.4 clearance holes each.
Shell bosses: diameter 10 with diameter-2.6 pilot holes; fastening details unvalidated.

EDITING AND REBUILDING
Parts are separate editable solids, not a single fused mesh.
smart_dumbbell_v01.lsp is the editable source. Load it and run SD_BUILD in a new empty drawing.
The builder refuses to run when the drawing already contains entities.
Save regenerated models under a new filename to preserve reviewed versions.

LIMITATIONS / NEXT DESIGN INPUTS
This is a packaging and presentation study, not a validated print-ready design.
All component envelopes are provisional. Measure the actual loan modules, display,
connectors, chosen power bank and their cables before freezing enclosure dimensions.
USB location assumes an internal extension; no cable routing assembly is provided.
No battery charging electronics, custom battery pack or electrical wiring is designed.
Grip retention, PCB brackets, power-bank retention, optical contact/shielding,
strain relief, edge finishing, print tolerances and structural loading need development.
No added exercise ballast is included. Do not infer a 1 kg product mass from this model.
Heart-rate measurement during movement remains a separate unverified requirement.
