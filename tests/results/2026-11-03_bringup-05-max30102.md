## Objective and predefined acceptance criterion

From `firmware/bringup/README.md` step 5: part ID 0x15; IR < 20000 without a finger and > 50000 with one; after 20 s the on-board `bpm_avg` within ±5 of a reference; finger removal clears the reading within 2 s.

## Setup and procedure

1. `03_i2c_scan` first: 0x57 appears alongside 0x1C and 0x6A.
2. Capture A: 5 s without a finger.
3. Capture B: 60 s at 25 Hz effective (100 sps, average 4), finger resting on the window.
4. Capture C: 70 s at 100 Hz effective (400 sps, average 4) after changing the sketch; finger placed about 20 s in.
5. Reference: Apple Watch heart-rate screen on the other wrist (values reported by Hanson; see below).

## Observations and measurements

- No finger: IR 515 to 550 counts, `finger=0`, bpm 0. Pass.
- Finger on: IR 50 000 to 125 000, `finger=1` immediately. Pass.
- Capture B (25 Hz): finger on 100 % of 60 s, DC about 115 000, pulsatile AC only 0.13 to 0.30 % of DC. On-board detector flagged 7 beats in 60 s; `bpm_avg` 26 to 47, wrong. Offline 15 s windows: spectrum 38/54/88/50 bpm, peak counting 72/60/88/92 bpm.
- Capture C (100 Hz): finger on 67 % of 70 s (placed late). On-board detector flagged 28 beats, median interval 95 bpm during the steady part; `bpm_avg` still swung 63 to 148 within 10 s windows. Offline on the steadiest window (30-45 s): AC/DC 0.35 %, peak counting about 100 bpm.
- Reference (Apple Watch): TO BE FILLED by Hanson for captures B and C.

## Result and limitations

- Outcome: Inconclusive. Sensor, wiring, I2C address and finger detection pass. Heart-rate value does not yet meet the ±5 bpm criterion.
- Evidence: captures summarised above; raw CSV kept outside the repository.
- Known limitations and causes identified:
  1. Pulsatile amplitude 0.1 to 0.4 % of DC is low (typical fingertip PPG 0.5 to 2 %). Finger pressure and placement dominate; no mechanical guide yet.
  2. SparkFun `checkForBeat()` expects ~100 Hz; at 25 Hz it missed most beats. Fixed in the sketch; at 100 Hz it still produces unstable intervals on this weak signal.
  3. The 4-beat running average in the sketch is too short to reject single bad intervals.
  4. Bus high level with the module's 1.8 V pull-ups was not measured; detection worked, so it is not blocking, but still measure it.
- Follow-up task: (a) fill in the watch readings; (b) replace the beat detector with a band-passed (0.7-3 Hz) peak picker or spectral estimate over a 8-10 s window and report validity as FR-08 requires; (c) design the grip window so the finger pad rests on the sensor with controlled light pressure; (d) only then plan AT-04 with a synchronized reference. The rest-only result above says nothing about heart rate during curls.
