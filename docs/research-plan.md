# Initial research assignments

## Researcher A: users and existing solutions

Read the supplied CCCARE project materials. Separate stated user needs from team assumptions. Compare three to four relevant products or alternatives, including a conventional dumbbell plus a paper/app log.

Deliver a table containing product, intended user, documented features, CAD price with date, interaction steps, relevant limitations and original source links. Summarize three evidence-supported needs and one design opportunity. Do not present assumed user preferences as interview findings.

## Researcher B: implementation and hardware

Investigate two to three concrete examples for motion counting, embedded classification, communication/display and optical pulse sensing. Record controller, sensor, method, available code, test evidence, reusable elements and unresolved compatibility issues.

Deliver a proposed borrowing/purchase list and the three highest-risk assumptions. Check the actual heart-rate library before attributing blocking delays to the hardware.

## Joint review

Confirm the specific user/problem, Version 1 functions, hardware rationale and first experiments. Keep the specification, BOM and pitch consistent. Prioritize manufacturer documentation, original code repositories and primary research over unsourced summaries.
